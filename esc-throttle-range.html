Throttle Range Setting

Remove all propellers. Disconnect battery power from every ESC before starting. Only power the selected ESC during this procedure.

Manual sequence

1. Keep the ESC battery disconnected, then press Start. Maximum throttle PWM will be applied.

2. Connect the ESC battery. Wait for self-test tone “123” and the battery-cell beeps. Then press Latch range. The controller waits two seconds for the two maximum-latched beeps and changes the signal to minimum within the manual's five-second window.

3. Wait approximately one second for the long beep confirming that minimum throttle was stored, then disconnect ESC battery power.

Ready. ESC battery must be disconnected.
