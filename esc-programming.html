ESC programming

Remove all propellers. Disconnect the selected ESC battery before starting. The motor must remain connected because it emits the menu tones.

Connect the ESC battery and listen

After two beeps and the “56712” entry tone, select an item exactly when its tone is announced. The eight tones repeat every three seconds.

Select value

Press the matching value while its one-, two-, or three-beep tone is audible.

Setting sent

Wait for confirmation tone “1515”. Then continue at full throttle, or exit at minimum throttle within two seconds.
